# Analytics screenshot · عقلك بياناتك

A one-page analytics dashboard for the **old two-booth games** (🧠 اعرف عقلك · Know Your Mind, 🔐 احمِ عقلك · Protect Your Mind),
in that site's look (Aref Ruqaa title, Cairo, violet glass cards). Period: 7–17 October 2026, when the old site was live.

The figures are **sample data**, not tracked results: 150 players, internally consistent across every card (funnel, the six
personalities, the four "Would You Share This?" messages, true/false accuracy, awareness levels, sources, devices). Edit the
arrays at the top of the `<script>` in `index.html` to change them.

- `analytics-screenshot.png`: the rendered page (1440 px wide at 2×).
- Re-render: open `index.html` in a browser, or screenshot it with Playwright at 1440 px, `deviceScaleFactor: 2`, full page.
